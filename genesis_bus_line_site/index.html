About Genesis Bus Line

Reliable transportation for schools, teams, companies, and tours.

Formerly known as Transtario, Genesis Bus Line carries forward a long-standing focus on safe, reliable, and well-coordinated group travel. From local school trips to North American motorcoach charters, our team keeps the experience simple from quote to drop-off.

Our work supports school groups, sports teams, corporate teams, wedding parties, religious organizations, inbound tourists, conventions, camps, and community clubs.
