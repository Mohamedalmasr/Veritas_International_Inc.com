Monthly Trust Account Checklist – Section 1: Month-End Cut-Off

Use this checklist each month to document completion and review of month-end cut-off procedures for the trust account.

1. Month-End Cut-Off

1.1 Ensure all trust transactions for the month are recorded in the trust ledger

1.2 Verify that all closed deals have been updated for trust movements

1.3 Obtain the complete month-end bank statement
